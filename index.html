Scoring rules

Normal: Make at least your declared groups but less than double. Score is declaration × 10, plus 1 per extra group.

Failed: Make fewer than declared, or double/more. Score is negative declaration × 10.

Andhi: Make 5 or more for +100. Otherwise −50.

Declarations: Each normal declaration must meet the match minimum, with no upper or combined-total limit.

Group total: Only actual groups must total exactly 13 before a game can be scored.
